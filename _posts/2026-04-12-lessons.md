I'm so glad that I've learned a lot. Looking back, I realize how naive I was a year ago. I still have a lot to learn, but I’m already excited to see what the future holds. Here are few key lessons I need to keep in mind for my own sake:

1. Pricing comes first. Understand the market and pricing (WTP validation) before proceeding with design and development. Sell first, then provide what customers want.

2. Is this product a _painkiller_ or a _vitamin_? Especially for founders going from 0 to 1: Does our product solve a real problem, or is it just a _nice-to-have_? Just because a product sounds good doesn’t mean it solves a problem or that people will pay for it. There are many good ideas, but people can live without them.

3. Targeting everyone means targeting no one. Trying to distribute to a broad audience dilutes the limited resources and focus. Paradoxically, the more widespread the problem, the more specific the initial target audience should be. 

4. Don’t overthink it. What we need isn’t our brain's thought, but the opinion of users. So stop trying to make it perfect and just ship it.

I'll keep the lessons updated as I learn more.
