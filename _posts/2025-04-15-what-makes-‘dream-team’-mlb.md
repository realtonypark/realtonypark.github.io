    <p>This research examines the relationship between advanced batting metrics and run production in Major League Baseball from 2015 to 2023. My research question is to investigate which batting metrics are most consistently correlated with team run totals during the regular season and how the relative importance of these metrics has changed since the introduction of Statcast technology in 2015. Specifically, I will analyze traditional metrics (such as batting average and on-base percentage) alongside advanced metrics (including exit velocity, hard-hit rate, and launch angle) to determine which are the strongest predictors of team offensive output.</p>
